About B. H. Khanov

B. H. Khanov is a debut author based in London, crafting explosive action thrillers and high-stakes contemporary fiction. With a mind for intricate plots and relentless pacing, he builds stories that refuse to tap the brakes.

Drawing inspiration from the gritty, hidden underworld of London and a fascination with high-stakes survival, he is currently putting the final touches on his highly anticipated debut action series.
